demo_naturalistic
=================

DEMO_NATURALISTIC  Voxelwise analysis of a continuous stimulus
--------------------------------------------------------------

Simulates a small fMRI cohort and runs a complete naturalistic correlation analysis. Participants are presented with a continuous stimulus (a film, a music excerpt, a spoken narrative); a feature time series extracted from that stimulus is correlated with the BOLD signal at every voxel, and the participant-level maps are tested at the group level.

**Pipeline steps:**

1. Simulate the cohort            fmri_simdata, fmri_simget
2. Build the BOLD regressor       fmri_doublegamma
3. Preprocess and correlate       fmri_detrend, fmri_correctmovement, fmri_zscorevoldata, fmri_corregressor
4. Group inference                fmri_ttest, fmri_p2t
5. Regions and visualisation      fmri_cleanclusters, fmri_regiontable, fmri_showslices, fmri_show3d

The simulated network contains both positive and negative responses to the stimulus feature. Their signs are stored in sim.design.network.sign. The two-tailed analysis keeps the two response directions separate.

All data are generated by the demo; no scanner data are required.
